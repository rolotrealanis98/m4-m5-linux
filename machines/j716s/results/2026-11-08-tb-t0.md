## What happened

The test payload was written from the daily (60 s settle) and the machine rebooted. It never answered again: no SSH,
no network, for 50+ minutes. Neither the self-test revert nor the stage-1 watchdog brought it back. Rolando
photographed the panel and power-cycled it.

## Results

| Feature / action | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Kernel init | fail | hung in a deferred probe for over 1000 s |
| Userspace, self-test, fallback | not reached | Linux had taken over the watchdog, so no reset happened |
| Thunderbolt / USB4 devices | not tested | |

## Log excerpts (from the photo of the panel)

```
INFO: task swapper/0:1 blocked for more than 1087 seconds.
wait_for_completion
__flush_work
flush_work
deferred_probe_initcall
do_one_initcall
kernel_init_freeable
```

Repeated at 1209 s and 1330 s.

## Analysis

A deferred driver probe blocked forever on a completion. Prime suspect: the USB4/CIO controller or the acio router
probe without the loader's combo-PHY tunables. Because the kernel feeds the watchdog from early boot, a hang in init is
not caught by the stage-1 fallback.

## What it needs before another attempt

A probe timeout on that path, and a boot-time watchdog that only completion of Linux's init pets. Until then Thunderbolt
payloads are attended only.
