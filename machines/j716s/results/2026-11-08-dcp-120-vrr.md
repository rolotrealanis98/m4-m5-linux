## Flip intervals (median page-flip interval)

| Phase | p50 |
|---|---|
| fixed | 13.2 ms |
| VRR on | 8.35 ms |
| VRR on, client paced at 50 fps | 20.8 ms |
| VRR off | 8.34 ms |
| VRR off, client paced at 50 fps | 16.65 ms |

With VRR on, a 50 fps client is shown at its own pace (real adaptive refresh); with VRR off it alternates 16.7/25 ms on
the fixed 120 Hz. The test program fills a 31 MB frame on the CPU, so its average fps and p95 outliers measure the
program, not the display.
