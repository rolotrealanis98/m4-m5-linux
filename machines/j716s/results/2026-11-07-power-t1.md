The first attempt of this payload (21:22 UTC) came back on the fallback; it followed a session of a different loader
and was later traced to the restart-while-busy problem (see the boot-safety result), not to these patches. The retest
(22:12 UTC) and the baseline (22:51 UTC) below ran after a plain reboot into the daily.

## Retest

`apple-cpufreq` on all three policies: min 300000, max 4308000 / 4308000 / 4608000 kHz, boost 4380000 listed on the
M clusters (boost off), latency 400 us; the hardware's current state reads back.

```
observation only: 5 agents, 4 rails, nothing is written
voter agent 1 is not enabled on rail 0/1
macsmc_soc_die: hottest of TCMb
```

Rail enables read 0 (the boot loader enables no voter); debugfs reports `mode: observe`.

## Baseline

Idle 60 s, 80-140 s after boot, battery full on the charger: SMC total system power mean 10.4 W. Under load on all 18
cores the CLPC computes its votes for all three clusters (4802 samples, every vote refused in observe mode) and the
frequency reaches 4308 / 4308 / 4608 MHz; the floors release afterwards.
