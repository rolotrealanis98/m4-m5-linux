## What was booted

Audio test payload A2 (`526ef58c`): the series audio kernel with the AOP audio stack, a device tree with the
microphones and the jack codec, speakers held off. Rolando was near the machine and spoke for the speech test.

## Results

| Feature / action | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Sound cards | pass | `AppleJ716AOP` (`t8140-aop-audio`) and `AppleJ716` (`macaudio`) |
| AOP | pass | RTKit protocol 12, 13 AFK endpoints, `aop-audio`, `aop-audprov`, `aop-voicetrigger` announced |
| Codecs | pass (probe) | `cs42l84` revision 36; six `tas2764` probed |
| Microphone, ambient | pass | RMS about -82 dBFS on all three capsules, not zeros |
| Microphone, speech | pass | RMS -71 dBFS, peaks -52 dBFS, per-second RMS following the voice, capsules within 0.2 dB |
| Headphone jack switches | registered | `Headphone Jack` and `Headset Mic Jack` present, both off |
| Headphones, headset mic | not tested | nothing plugged in |
