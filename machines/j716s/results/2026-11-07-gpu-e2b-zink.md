## Daily promotion

First boot on the primary slot, 76 s to SSH, 18 CPUs, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, haptics, two sound cards, battery;
`GPU firmware: 27.3 (26C5035r), G17S`, `[drm] Initialized aurora 1.0.0`. The session launcher starts the GPU session
when the `aurora` card and the Mesa tree exist, and falls back to the software session if the GPU session dies within
30 s.
