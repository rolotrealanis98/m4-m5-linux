What happened #

The test payload was written from the daily (60 s settle) and the machine rebooted. It never answered again: no SSH, no network, for 50+ minutes. Neither the self-test revert nor the stage-1 watchdog brought it back. Rolando photographed the panel and power-cycled it.

Analysis #

A deferred driver probe blocked forever on a completion. Prime suspect: the USB4/CIO controller or the acio router probe without the loader's combo-PHY tunables. Because the kernel feeds the watchdog from early boot, a hang in init is not caught by the stage-1 fallback.

What it needs before another attempt #

A probe timeout on that path, and a boot-time watchdog that only completion of Linux's init pets. Until then Thunderbolt payloads are attended only.
