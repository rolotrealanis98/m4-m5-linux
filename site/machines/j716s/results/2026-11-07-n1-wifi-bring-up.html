The first Wi-Fi and Bluetooth bring-up on the J716s, with m1n1 training the N1's PCIe port and the kernel adopting it. Three runs: W3b (13:44 UTC, cold start by Rolando), DAILY-WIFI-T1 (14:00 UTC, nobody at the machine) and W4 (15:37 UTC, restarts with the link in use). The current daily trains the port in the kernel instead (see the K1/K2 result).

W3b #

pcie-t6050: port 0 link Gen1 x1
endpoint 01:00.0 is 0x1900106b
apple_centauri: enabling device
port 0: link retrained after a root-port reset
Wi-Fi firmware channels: 2.4 GHz 13, 5 GHz 25, 6 GHz 0 (38 entries)

Functions [106b:1900], [106b:1902] and [106b:1903] enumerate; wlan0 (phy0, managed) and hci0 (Bluetooth version 17) register, rfkill clear; up past 123 s with ASPM off. Radio check on the same boot: iw dev wlan0 scan lists 55 BSS entries (33 networks) on 2.4 and 5 GHz; btmgmt find for 8 s finds 36 devices.

DAILY-WIFI-T1 #

wpa_supplicant with a configuration written by wpa_passphrase (the passphrase is only on the machine's root): wpa_state=COMPLETED, WPA2-PSK, 5 GHz (5200 MHz), signal -30 dBm, DHCPv4 lease and gateway from the home router, 3 of 3 pings answered over wlan0 (17 ms average). Bluetooth controller up.

W4 #

Three systemctl reboot cycles with wlan0 associated and a ping running: all three back on the primary slot, N1 adopted, wlan0 associated again. The pcie-apple shutdown that parks the adopted port (edimoldovan's, from the J714c) is what makes the restart work.

Not tested #

6 GHz (no 6 GHz entries in this regulatory list), Bluetooth pairing, throughput.
