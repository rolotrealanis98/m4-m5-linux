Display (DCP) on the T6050 #

What is known #

  • The display coprocessor firmware comes from macOS like the GPU's. Its method numbers and swap layouts differ between firmware 27.x builds and are not interchangeable: 27.3 beta 1 is 27.2 plus one method and a larger swap request. Unknown firmware builds should be refused rather than treated as another version.
  • The loader hands over the running DCP and the framebuffer iBoot set up (omacom/m1n1 #35), and the firmware-27 driver negotiates with it instead of restarting it.
  • The device-tree node needs the internal panel's bandwidth scratch offset (apple,bw-scratch) or the driver refuses the tree, and the display clock of the disp0 pipe (not the empty clock list of the dcp node) or the firmware complains about an undeclared clock provider.
  • Swap timestamps: counter-based timestamps on 120 Hz swaps stalled the first swap after the mode set; constant timestamps fix it.
  • Modes: the J716s firmware offers six timings at 3456x2234 (120 Hz preferred, 60, 59.94, 50, 48, 47.95). VRR is an opt-in and changes the measured flip interval.
  • Formats: betizzel reports that on the J714s the firmware treats the X byte of XRGB as alpha, so the console and compositor must use alpha formats (a Hyprland change was needed).
  • Brightness: on the J716s writes are accepted but do not reach the panel yet; edimoldovan (J714c) and betizzel (J714s) report working brightness control.

Status by board #

  • J716s: works on test payloads; the first daily candidate with the DCP and the GPU hand-off together panicked early and is being bisected.
  • J714c, J714s: the DCP drives the internal panel (reported).

Results and PRs #

  • J716s: stage C, 120 Hz and VRR, daily candidate fallback.
  • PRs: aurora-silicon/linux #1174 (27.3 method table and swap layout), #1012; omacom/m1n1 #35.
