Power: idle, frequency, sleep and the SMC on the T6050 #

CPU idle #

  • With idle=nop every core spins: about 16 W at idle on the J716s and 15 W on the J714s.
  • WFI loses general-purpose and FP/SIMD state on the non-boot cores unless the kernel preserves registers across it. With Aurora's register-preserving idle path the cores really sleep: J716s 16.4 W to about 9.8 W on the charger, J714s 15.3 W to 5.6 W (betizzel). Keep arm64.nowfxt and kvm-arm.wfi_trap_policy=trap.
  • m1n1 still appends idle=nop; the J716s kernel ignores it for WFI (aurora-silicon/linux #1152).

Frequency #

  • Three clusters, three cpufreq policies. Without OPP tables for clusters 1 and 2 the cores run where the firmware left them (J714s P cores measured at 1307 MHz). The ADT's voltage-state tables give the steps; boost states are marked turbo-mode on the J716s.
  • betizzel measured on the J714s that hardware p-state N runs at ADT table entry N-2; check scaling_cur_freq against a measured clock on other boards.
  • The power-management controller (PMC) and the CLPC can be observed without writing anything (J716s test kernel); writing floors is the next, attended step. The PMP coprocessor is reported running on the J714c.

Sleep #

  • s2idle works with the lid as a wake source through macsmc-input; a lid-open report during sleep is confirmed against the SMC before it wakes the machine (aurora-silicon/linux #1160).
  • The adopted N1 port must stay up through s2idle (see n1-wireless).
  • J714s: an unused debug UART faulted on the second suspend in a boot until its power domain was taken around the resume (betizzel).

SMC #

  • Battery and charger through macsmc-power: its work items must be initialised before the power-supply notifier, or the probe hangs the machine.
  • hwmon: the T6050 fan mode keys are F0md/F1md; there is no fan-control unlock key.

Results and PRs #

  • J716s: WFI idle and sleep, lid sleep, battery, sensors, PMC observe.
  • PRs: aurora-silicon/linux #1152, #1160, #1084, #481 (T6050 cpufreq profile, held discussion).
