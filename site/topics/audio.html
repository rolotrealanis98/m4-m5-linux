Audio on the T6050 MacBook Pros #

Pieces #

  • Microphones sit behind the AOP coprocessor (t8140-aop-audio): three capsules on the J716s, recorded as a three-channel float stream.
  • Speakers: six TAS2764 amplifiers on the speaker link, a jack codec (CS42L84), and speakersafetyd guarding the amplifiers; the kernel keeps the volumes locked until the daemon runs.
  • The speaker volume ceiling is set in the kernel (snd_soc_macaudio.speaker_max_db); the J716s daily uses -30 dB.

Board differences #

  • J716s: the speaker link only runs clean in the J616s TDM frame; with the J714 serializer settings every amplifier logged bad TDM clock. edimoldovan reports no such fault on the J714c with the J714 settings, so this is specific to the J716s link.
  • Apple's J716s speaker tuning is byte-identical to the J616s one, so the J616s DSP graph is the base of a J716s profile (built, not yet listened to).
  • A udev rule for speakersafetyd must match all keys on one parent device, or it never fires and the speakers stay silent (the safe failure).

Results and PRs #

  • J716s: microphone, speakers.
  • J714c: speakers and microphone reported working (machines/j714c).
  • PRs: omacom/omarchy-mac-pkgs #103 (J716s in the audio tests and the manual).
