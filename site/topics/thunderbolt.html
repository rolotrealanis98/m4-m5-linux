Thunderbolt and USB4 on the T6050 #

Status #

  • Aurora Silicon's J716c work (aurora-silicon/linux #946) adds Thunderbolt 5 and USB4 on the right USB-C port, including a hardware-checked CIO reset sequence.
  • J716s T0 (blocked): a kernel with USB4/Thunderbolt built in and the ATC1/ATC2 routers, PCIe tunnels, DARTs and NHIs in the device tree hung in kernel init: swapper/0 blocked in deferred_probe_initcall -> flush_work for over 1000 s. Userspace and the self-test never ran, and because Linux had taken over the watchdog nothing reset the machine.
  • Prime suspect: a router or controller probe that waits forever without the loader's combo-PHY tunables.

Before anyone tries again #

  • Add a timeout on that probe path, and use a boot-time watchdog that only Linux's init completion pets.
  • Run Thunderbolt payloads attended only, with a power cycle and the macOS recovery path ready.
  • Anything a router probe needs from the loader (tunables) should be checked on a USB-only boot first.
